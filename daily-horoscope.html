Daily Horoscope

Enter your birth date to create a dated personal horoscope that combines today’s Personal Day number with your tropical Sun sign. You will see the arithmetic, practical reflection prompts, daily focus areas and the exact date of the reading, without claims of live planetary transits or guaranteed predictions.

How is today’s horoscope calculated?

The numerology part calculates the Personal Year, Personal Month and Personal Day from your birth month, birth day and today's date. The astrology part identifies only your tropical Sun sign from your birth date. It does not claim to calculate live planetary transits.

  1. Enter your birth date.
  2. The app calculates your Personal Year, Personal Month and Personal Day.
  3. The birth date identifies your tropical Sun sign.
  4. The report combines the two interpretations while keeping their calculations separate.
  5. Return on another date to generate a newly dated insight.

Questions about today’s horoscope?

Does the daily horoscope use live planetary positions?

No. The daily horoscope combines a transparent numerology Personal Day calculation with general tropical Sun-sign symbolism. It does not calculate the current positions of planets, houses, aspects or transits. That distinction appears inside the result so users can understand exactly what the tool does. Treat the reading as an entertainment feature rather than a forecast of events. Important decisions about health, money, work, relationships or legal matters should rely on evidence, personal judgement and appropriate professional advice, not a symbolic daily reading.

Why does my daily result change each day?

The Personal Day number uses the current calendar date, so its final value can change when the date changes. Your Life Path and Sun sign remain based on your birth details, while the Personal Year, Personal Month and Personal Day represent different time scales. The page displays the date attached to the reading to prevent an older result from appearing current. Revisit the tool on a new day to generate a fresh calculation, and open the calculation details whenever you want to verify the arithmetic yourself.

What information is stored when I create a daily horoscope?

The daily tool is designed to perform its calculation in the browser. A birth date or optional first name is not placed in the public page address. Browser storage may be used only where the site clearly explains a save function or necessary preference. Do not enter sensitive information that the form does not request. Review the Privacy Policy and Data Deletion page for the current handling rules, and clear saved browser data whenever you no longer want information retained on that particular device.

How should I use the love, career and money prompts?

Use the topic prompts as questions for reflection, not instructions or promises. A favourable-looking career rating cannot guarantee an interview, promotion or business result, and a relationship prompt cannot determine compatibility or another person’s behaviour. The ratings are generated from the same disclosed symbolic framework as the rest of the daily horoscope. Consider your actual circumstances, responsibilities and evidence first. The prompts are most useful when they help you pause, organise a thought or identify one sensible action you were already able to evaluate independently.

Can I listen to or print today’s reading?

The complete chart includes audio controls and a print or PDF option, while the dedicated daily page provides a concise dated result for quick use. Browser speech voices depend on the device and operating system, so the exact voice can vary. Printing may also differ slightly between browsers. For the strongest printable report, create the complete chart and use its final report controls after reviewing every section. The print stylesheet uses darker text and simplified backgrounds to improve legibility on paper and in a saved PDF.
