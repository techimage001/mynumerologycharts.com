Questions about the Name Checker

Is the score a prediction?

No. It shows how a name number pairs with birth numbers under traditional numerology families. It is symbolic and for entertainment. It cannot tell you how a child, a business or a marriage will turn out, and a name you love and can live with matters more than any score.

How is the score worked out?

Each name gives an Expression number (all letters), a Soul Urge (vowels) and a Personality number (consonants). These are paired with the Life Path and Birthday number from the date. Same number scores 100, same root 95, same family (1-5-7, 2-4-8, 3-6-9) 82 and contrast 55. The weighted result is shown on a 40 to 100 scale, so contrast is never presented as failure.

Does choosing boy or girl change the score?

No. Numerology works only from the letters and the date, so the numbers are the same whoever the name is for. Choosing boy or girl labels each name and decides which names the middle-name finder suggests. Labels come from public birth records covering about 16,000 names; a name outside that list is shown as not in our list rather than guessed.

Pythagorean or Chaldean?

Pythagorean is the system used everywhere else on this site and is the default. Chaldean uses a different letter chart and is popular in India. The two can give different numbers for the same name, which is expected. Our guide to why calculators disagree explains it.

Is it really free?

Yes. The ranking, the full working for every name, the respellings, the middle-name finder and the image and PDF downloads are all free. There is no account to create.

Do you store the names I type?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. Images and PDFs are made on your device. If you share a link, the names travel inside the link itself and the date is only included if you tick the box.
